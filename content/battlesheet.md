# Sales Battlesheet

## Ideal customer profiles

Local businesses in Oeiras with a strong service, an inconsistent digital presence, and a clear need for qualified local demand.

## Core pain points

- Low local visibility
- Outdated websites or unclear positioning
- Inconsistent lead flow
- No simple way to measure digital acquisition

## Outreach angles

Lead with a specific observation, tie the gap to a business outcome, and offer a small concrete next step.

## Objections

Address existing suppliers by finding uncovered gaps. For low priority, quantify the cost of waiting and agree on a follow-up date.

## Qualification rules

Confirm decision-maker access, a real problem, commercial intent, realistic timing, and sufficient engagement value.

## Pricing notes

Anchor pricing to scope and expected impact. Keep the initial offer narrow and easy to approve.

## Follow-up logic

Follow up after 3 business days, then 7 days. Add value on each touch. Pause after the third unanswered message.
